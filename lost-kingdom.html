Lost Kingdom

Open

A green island on a brown cliff, with vines hanging down its sides.

A big blue dinosaur with purple spots stands by a stone gate. Butterflies drift over the palm trees.

i In the game, you need 1 golden key to get in.

In OpenTPW today

This is the land that works in OpenTPW today. You can open the park the game comes with.

  • Guests: They arrive, queue, ride and buy drinks.
  • Building: You can lay paths, and buy and sell rides.
  • Staff: You can hire them, and set a training budget for each kind.
  • Breakdowns: Rides wear out and break down. A mechanic walks over and mends them.
  • Advisor: He warns you about problems in your park. Some of his tags open the screen that fixes them.
  • Saving and finishing: Not yet. A park cannot be saved, and a game cannot be finished.

OpenTPW is a fan project. It rebuilds the game Theme Park World. In America the game is called Sim Theme Park.

It holds no game files. You need your own copy of the game to play.

It is not made or approved by the game's makers or publishers.

OpenTPW was started by Alex Guthrie and its contributors. This site follows a fork of their work. The original project on GitHub
