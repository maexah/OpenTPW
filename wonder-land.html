OpenTPW is a fan project. It rebuilds the game Theme Park World. In America the game is called Sim Theme Park.

It holds no game files. You need your own copy of the game to play.

It is not made or approved by the game's makers or publishers.

OpenTPW was started by Alex Guthrie and its contributors. This site follows a fork of their work. The original project on GitHub
