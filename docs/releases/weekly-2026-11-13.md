**Second experimental build.** Unfinished. Needs your own copy of the game. Back up your `save` folder. Setup is in `README.txt`.

**Playable:** Lost Kingdom. The other three lands aren't ready.

**New**
- Litter: guests drop it, walk to bins, and it is drawn and saved
- A park six months in the red ends
- Ride window: Buy Upgrades, the call-a-mechanic button, the status box in its colour, the track button only on track rides
- Mechanics carry out bought upgrades
- Sideshow window: its settings and figures
- Park map: its six switches and two pickers
- A sold coaster's pylons come down
- Guests: a happy guest jumps; guests turned back at a closed gate; leavers change their mind when the park reopens
- Every list has a scrollbar and opens with its first row picked
- Camera: the wheel zooms in steps as the original's, first person turns at the original's rates, the orbit leans as it pans
- Lightning, weather carried on from a loaded park, the fly-in's fade to black
- People's shadows at high detail; the ground, paths, sea and interface drawn one-sided as the original's
- VSync and a frame limiter, set in `opentpw.cfg` (no Options row yet)
- Button clicks sound as the original's on the map, options and door buttons
- A settings file that is backed up and replaced now says so; unknown lines in `opentpw.cfg` are kept
- Many smaller fixes to saving, sounds, scripts and the advisor

**Not yet**
- Coaster track
- Saving a bought coaster
- Loans
- The park's end screen
- Five of the nine object windows
- Map overlay and clicks

**Platforms:** Windows, Linux, and Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). The Mac build is untested.

**No Intel Mac build this time.** GitHub's Mac build machines were full all day, so it could not be made. It should be back with the next build. Until then, the [previous build](https://github.com/maexah/OpenTPW/releases/tag/weekly-2026-11-01) has an Intel Mac zip.

**Network:** checks GitHub for a newer build at start. Nothing is downloaded. `README.txt` says how to turn it off.

**Report a problem:** [Issues](https://github.com/maexah/OpenTPW/issues), "Weekly build problem", attach `console.log`.
