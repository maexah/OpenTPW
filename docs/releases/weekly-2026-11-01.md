**First experimental build.** Unfinished. Needs your own copy of the game. Back up your `save` folder. Setup is in `README.txt`.

**Playable:** Lost Kingdom. The other three lands aren't ready.

**Works**
- Lobby, players, saves, options
- Guests, staff, and 23 of Lost Kingdom's 31 rides, shops and sideshows
- Building paths, queues and rides
- Kart and water ride track
- Park management screens, research, golden tickets
- Save and load, including parks saved by the original game

**Not yet**
- Coaster track
- Saving a bought coaster
- Loans
- Litter
- Five of the nine object windows
- Map overlay and clicks

**Platforms:** Windows, Linux, macOS. The Mac builds are untested.

**Network:** checks GitHub for a newer build at start. Nothing is downloaded. `README.txt` says how to turn it off.

**Report a problem:** [Issues](https://github.com/maexah/OpenTPW/issues), "Weekly build problem", attach `console.log`.
